ABOUT MARINEX SHIPPING LINE

Built on Chattogram's docks, trading across the region.

Marinex Shipping Line operates as a Shipping Liner agency and NVOCC across regional container trade — connecting the Far East, the Indian subcontinent, and the UAE through Chattogram and Mongla. We work both sides of the manifest: representing main line operators and running our own consolidated NVOCC services.

Alongside shipping, we run a freight forwarding desk covering sea and air cargo, and a feeder division adding new regional destinations as demand grows. Every shipment is handled by people who know the ports, not just the paperwork.

Headquartered in Chattogram, with a liaison office in Dhaka
WHAT WE DO

Six ways we move your cargo

From a single container booking to a full freight-forwarding contract, our services are built around the regional trade lanes we know best.

Shipping Liner Agency

Scheduled liner routes for main line operators, run with fixed sailing schedules across our core Far East and subcontinent lanes.

NVOCC Operations

Consolidated container shipping under our own bills of lading, giving smaller shippers liner-level reach without a full charter.

Freight Forwarding

Door-to-port and port-to-door coordination — documentation, customs liaison, and inland haulage handled under one desk.

Sea & Air Freight

Ocean freight for volume cargo and air freight for time-critical shipments, quoted and booked from the same team.

Feeder Services

Regular feeder connections to Singapore, Port Klang and Colombo, with new destinations added as regional demand grows.

Warehousing & Distribution

Short and long-term container and cargo storage near port, with inland distribution arranged on request.
